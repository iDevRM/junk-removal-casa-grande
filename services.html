Furniture & Mattress Removal

Sofas, sectionals, recliners, bed frames, dressers, dining sets, mattresses and box springs. These are the items that jam a hallway, do not fit in a pickup bed, and take two people to move without wrecking a doorframe.

Left alone in an Arizona garage, upholstered furniture becomes a pest problem fast — heat and stored fabric attract roaches and rodents within a season. Mattresses are worse, because most Pinal County drop-offs charge a separate fee to take them and some will not take them at all.

Anything still in usable shape gets pulled aside for donation instead of going to the landfill. Read more about furniture and mattress removal in Casa Grande.

Appliance Removal

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, ranges, dishwashers, water heaters and window AC units. Appliances are heavy, sharp-edged and awkward on stairs, and the refrigerant ones carry rules a homeowner cannot legally skip.

Refrigerators, freezers and AC units hold refrigerant that has to be recovered by a certified technician before the metal can be scrapped. The City of Casa Grande charges a fee per refrigerant appliance whether you set it at the curb or haul it to the landfill yourself. Ignoring that step is how a dead fridge ends up sitting in a side yard through August.

A failed water heater is its own emergency — it is still full of water and usually sitting in a closet with no clear path out. See how appliance removal works in Casa Grande.

Yard Waste & Construction Debris

Mesquite and palo verde limbs, palm fronds, ripped-out landscaping, fence panels, patio covers, old cabinets, carpet, tile, drywall and shed teardowns. Dense material that hits a weight limit long before it fills a trailer.

Monsoon season is the driver. Storms roll through Pinal County from roughly July into September and take down fence sections, shade structures and half-rotted limbs in one night. The pile that lands in a back yard after a storm is exactly the kind of thing the City turns down as household bulk trash.

Remodel debris has the same problem year-round. Construction material is not household waste, so it needs a separate disposal run. Read about yard and construction debris removal in Casa Grande.

What We Cannot Take

Household hazardous waste stays with Pinal County collection programs, not with us. That means paint and solvents, pesticides and pool chemicals, motor oil and antifreeze, propane tanks, medical sharps, and ammunition.

If you are unsure about an item, call before the crew arrives. It saves everyone a trip and we will point you at the right place to take it.
